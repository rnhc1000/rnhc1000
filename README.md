### About Me :sunglasses:
Hi everyone! I am a Software Engineer :hammer_and_wrench: who loves to design, code and deploy applications. I have been very successful in being the architect of solutions that have met and even exceeded business and customer requirements in all companies I have been working for, since the very beginning of my career as a Junior Engineer. Playing in this market for almost 25 years :detective:, I have been exposed to a myriad of technologies and been a very early adopter of some. My first contact with a programming language was with Pascal in university, where I learned key programming concepts. My first professional job involved Assembler (8085, 8086, Z-80) and C languages, building device drivers for mass storage on x86 platforms (XT and AT IBM PCs), using Turbo C and Turbo Assembler :man_technologist:. Moved to networking to surf the Internet wave in the 90s, obtained a couple of Cisco certifications, then transitioned to Web development with LAMP :woozy_face:. Returned to university :man_student: for a Software Engineering postgraduate degree, diving into OOP, Java, and Spring Framework. Currently working with JAva, Spig Boot, Spring framework, Spark, Snowplow, AWS, APIs, CI/CD, Jenkins, SonarQube, and others  to deliver secure financial applications. Visit https://ferreiras.dev.br :sunglasses: for more about my career. The image above :construction_worker_man: reflects what I love most :two_hearts:: building web applications in first-class team environments. Enjoy this repository! :partying_face:
